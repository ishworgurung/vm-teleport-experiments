EXPERIMENT  Look inside the restored TCP socket, in the case where its replies never arrive
QUESTION    After CRIU restores a client with --tcp-established, does the guest kernel's TCP socket still look healthy while the client's requests time out?
HOW         The same case as run 3 of 'make criu-net'. While the restored client runs, the guest samples the socket with 'ss -tin' after 1, 4 and 10 seconds. ss prints the kernel's own counters for the connection, for example how many bytes it has acknowledged and how many data segments it has received.
